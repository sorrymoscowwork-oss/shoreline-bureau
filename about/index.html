About us

Independent property advice.
Your interests first.

SHORELINE BUREAU is an independent property advisory bureau focused on Hurghada and the surrounding Red Sea region. We help international buyers understand the local market, assess properties and navigate the process from the initial search through to completion.

Why work with SHORELINE BUREAU

Buying property in another country involves much more than choosing an apartment or a house. You need to understand the different areas, prices, developers, documentation, transaction terms and the people you will be dealing with.

Without local market knowledge, it is easy to spend time considering unsuitable properties, make decisions based on incomplete information or accept terms that could have been improved. It is not always obvious which questions to ask, what needs to be checked before signing or where to find reliable, independent support.

We help you navigate the process with a clear understanding of the local market and professional support on your side.

Rather than having to research an unfamiliar market, manage multiple negotiations and assess every claim on your own, you gain guidance and a clearer picture of the situation. We help narrow down the options, examine important details, compare proposals and focus on the factors that may affect the cost and security of your purchase.

This helps protect two valuable resources: your money and your time. Both can be easily wasted when buying property abroad without the right knowledge and support.

Independence is at the core of what we do

SHORELINE BUREAU is not a real estate agency and does not represent developers, sellers or intermediaries. We have no agreements with specific companies, no obligation to promote particular properties and no financial interest in which seller you choose.

We do not accept commissions, referral fees or undisclosed payments from market participants. Our fee is paid by the client and agreed in advance.

This allows us to assess properties and proposals objectively and examine all the circumstances surrounding a purchase. We are not here to push you into a deal at any cost. If a property does not meet your requirements, its price appears unjustified, the terms raise concerns or risks emerge during the process, we will tell you. If a seller or intermediary has a questionable reputation, we will bring it to your attention.

We carefully examine every important aspect of a transaction: the property itself, its price, the purchase terms, the reputation of the seller or developer, the documentation and potential risks. Our role is to understand the full picture and help you make informed decisions at every stage of the purchase.

We work on the buyer’s side

We help you define your search criteria, understand the available options and assess them in light of your goals and budget.

Where needed, we take part in negotiations with sellers or developers, help discuss the price and terms, arrange checks of key aspects of the transaction and coordinate with independent legal professionals.

Our role is to help ensure that decisions are made with a clear understanding of the facts and that your interests are considered throughout the process — from the first viewing to receiving the property documents.

We do not encourage you to buy simply for the sake of completing a transaction. Sometimes the most sensible decision is to continue looking or walk away from a particular offer.

Clear terms and a transparent fee

Our fee is agreed in advance and depends on the scope of the task. It is a fixed fee for a consultation, property search or transaction support, depending on the service format you choose.

Our income does not depend on the property price, the discount negotiated or the seller you ultimately choose. This allows us to focus on what matters to the buyer: finding suitable property, securing properly assessed terms and making a well-informed purchase decision.

We remain available after the purchase

Our work does not necessarily end when the transaction is completed. Clients who have used our full purchase support service can contact us for six months after completion for complimentary legal and other consultations relating to their property.

We believe it is important that, after the purchase, you still have a point of contact with people who know the background of your transaction and can help you navigate any questions that arise.
